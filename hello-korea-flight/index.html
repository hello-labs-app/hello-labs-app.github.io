WHY A SEPARATE APP?

Focus on the flight.
Keep the trip connected.

Hello Korea Flight is being built around the air-travel part of a Korea trip. A working Android development project and release-build APK already exist. Keeping it separate lets the main Hello Korea app stay focused on the experience at your destination.

Project stage: in development

No public launch date has been announced for Flight. The January 2027 target on this website refers to the main Hello Korea app.

INSIDE THE DEVELOPMENT PROJECT

A clearer flight experience.

The project includes the features below. It is not a public store release. Data coverage and live operation can vary, and this website does not provide a live flight service.

01

Your flights, kept close

Save flights and keep notes and a checklist alongside your journey. The current development app focuses on a personal flight overview without requiring a booking import.

02

An airport view

Check the Incheon departure and arrival board and find airport essentials. The current board scope is Incheon, not a claim of complete live coverage at every airport.

03

A view of the sky

Explore the live-aircraft map around Incheon, Gimpo, Gimhae and Jeju. Aircraft visibility and information freshness depend on the available data.

WHERE THINGS STAND

Built so far.
Still moving forward.

In the current project

  • Saved flights, notes and checklist
  • Incheon departure and arrival board
  • Aircraft map around ICN, GMP, PUS and CJU
  • Airport essentials
  • Light and dark appearance

Not available yet

  • Account synchronization between apps
  • Push alerts while the app is closed
  • Booking imports
  • Public app-store release

Signing in through Hello Korea and importing travel information with consent are planned connections, not current released features.

A useful companion,
not your airline’s final word.

The intended app experience is informational. For boarding, gate changes, delays and travel decisions, always follow the airline and airport’s official guidance. No live flight service is provided by this website.
