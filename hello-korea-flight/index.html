WHY A SEPARATE APP?

Focus on the flight.
Keep the trip connected.

Hello Korea Flight is being shaped around the air-travel part of a Korea trip. Keeping it separate lets the main Hello Korea app stay focused on the experience at your destination.

Project stage: planning and development

No public launch date has been announced for Flight. The January 2027 target on this website refers to the main Hello Korea app.

WHAT WE’RE WORKING TOWARD

A clearer flight experience.

The following describes the intended direction, not released or verified functionality.

01

Korea-focused flights

A focused experience for finding and following flights relevant to a Korea journey. Final route coverage and available information will depend on the data sources used.

02

Flight and airport context

Bring flight information, aircraft positions and airport-map context closer together where reliable data is available. Coverage, freshness and gate accuracy will need validation.

03

A connection you control

The planned link with Hello Korea would let travellers sign in through the main app and import travel information only after giving consent in Flight.

A useful companion,
not your airline’s final word.

The intended app experience is informational. For boarding, gate changes, delays and travel decisions, always follow the airline and airport’s official guidance. No live flight service is provided by this website.
