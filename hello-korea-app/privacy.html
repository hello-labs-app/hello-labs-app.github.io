Hello Korea Privacy Policy

This policy is for the Hello Korea Android app. The Hello Labs website privacy notice is separate.

Who we are and how to contact us

Hello Korea is operated by Hello Labs. Our privacy officer is Jun-ho Eum (엄준호). For privacy questions or requests, email contact.hello.labs@gmail.com.

Information we use and why

  • Sign-in: Firebase Authentication uses your email address and account identifier for email sign-in, and sends a link to verify control of your mailbox. Google sign-in provides your name, email and identity token. Kakao sign-in provides your account identifier, nickname and any email you authorize. Older app versions may use one-time email codes delivered through Cloudflare and Gmail.
  • Travel tools: Information you add to your travel profile, trips, notes, expenses, checklists and saved places is stored on your device and is not synced through your Hello Korea account.
  • Maps and place search: Cloudflare and Kakao receive search terms, categories and the area you view. Your current coordinates are sent only when you choose nearby search and grant permission. Hello Korea does not track location in the background. You can search by name without granting location access.
  • Weather and currency: The service uses your selected city or region to request weather; it does not use device GPS for weather. Our server retrieves public weather and exchange-rate data and does not send your account details or itinerary to those data sources.
  • Service and support: Network services receive IP and request information to deliver and secure requests. If you email us, we use your address and the information you send to respond and handle your request.

The current Android release has no ad placements and does not start ad requests or the advertising consent flow. It includes Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform libraries. See Google's SDK data disclosure for details about what these libraries can process when used.

Service providers and processing locations

Kakao in South Korea provides optional Kakao sign-in and place search. Google provides Google sign-in and email services. Firebase Authentication processes authentication data in the United States. Email and support information may be processed where Google operates. Cloudflare provides API, email verification and security services over its global network; processing is not limited to South Korea. You may decline optional online features or revoke location permission in device settings, and you can still use the app's local travel tools.

Retention, security and deletion

App API connections use HTTPS. Older email verification codes are stored in hashed form, expire after 10 minutes and are subject to sending and attempt limits. Google describes its authentication log and deletion retention in the Firebase privacy documentation.

We keep ordinary support correspondence for 30 days after the request is resolved. We may retain it longer if required by law or while a related dispute remains active.

To remove information stored on your device, use Profile → Settings → Data on this device. Deleting local data does not remove an online authentication account.

To request account deletion, use the account-deletion option in the app or email contact.hello.labs@gmail.com with the subject Hello Korea account deletion. We verify that the request concerns your account. Do not email a password or verification code. Deleting your Hello Korea account does not delete your Google, Kakao or email-provider account.

Your choices and rights

You can use local travel tools without signing in and can revoke location access in Android settings. You may email us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or withdrawal of consent, subject to applicable law.

Children and changes

Hello Korea is intended for people aged 14 and older. If you believe a child under 14 has provided information, contact us so we can investigate. We will describe material changes to this policy in the app or on this page.
